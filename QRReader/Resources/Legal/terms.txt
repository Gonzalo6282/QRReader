## What the App Does
The App reads QR codes and barcodes with your iPhone's camera or from photos and files, shows what they contain, checks links on your iPhone, keeps a history on your iPhone and lets you create QR codes.

## Free Features and Pro
Scanning codes and seeing what they contain is free. Qodi Pro unlocks actions on your scans (such as opening links, joining Wi-Fi networks, adding contacts and events, calling, messaging, emailing, copying and sharing), batch scanning with CSV export, the full scan history, opening safe links automatically, and saving and sharing the codes you create.
